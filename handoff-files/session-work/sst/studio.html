A complete editor, made for events.

From the first cut to the finished file, without leaving the app.

Multicam from the event

Lumora's recordings open with every angle synced. Switch cameras as it plays, or nudge a camera a frame at a time.

Timeline editing

Ripple, roll, slip, slide, razor, insert and overwrite. As many tracks as you need, with keyframes and a graph editor.

Node color grading

Serial, parallel and layer nodes. Color wheels, primaries, curves and LUTs. Copy a grade to every clip from the same camera.

Scopes

Waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope and histogram, live beside the picture while you grade.

Audio mixer and voice cleanup

Faders, pan and meters for every track. EQ, compressor and noise reduction make a speech in a loud hall clear.

Captions and transcript

Transcribe on your own computer, then edit the video by editing the text. Translate captions and export SRT or VTT.

Smart tools

Remove silences and filler words, build a multicam edit automatically, reframe for vertical and square, and cut a highlight reel.

AI masks and tracking

Select a person or an object with one click. Track a point or a region so a title, blur or mask follows it.

Speed, titles and templates

Speed changes and ramps, animated titles, transitions, green screen and ready-made templates in your event's look.

Team review

Share a project with your team. Leave comments on an exact frame and mark them done as the edit comes together.

Fast playback

Hardware decoding, native GPU playback, proxies and a render cache keep long multicam events smooth.

Export presets

YouTube up to 4K, Shorts, Reels, TikTok, ProRes, DNxHR, WAV and MP3, or a file ready to play back in Lumora.
