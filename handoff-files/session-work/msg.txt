Lumora Edit: transcribe, captions, text-based editing and voice cleanup

Transcribe (offline): selected clips or the whole sequence are written
down by Lumora's own Whisper code (shared from app/src/captions, run in a
worker), base or small model, any language or worked out from what is
said first. The editor downloads the model once with progress (or uses
Lumora's copy) and reads each file's sound at 16 kHz through FFmpeg.
Speech is cut into stretches at pauses; word times are shared out over
the talking moments. Transcripts are kept on the media files.

Captions: a captions track (a video track that holds caption blocks and
one look: font, size, weight, color, outline, box, shadow, place, letters
a line, lines). Blocks are made from the transcript, edited in the
inspector, split at the playhead and joined; drawn by the compositor in
playback and export (burn-in can be turned off), and saved as .srt and
.vtt from the Captions menu or next to an exported film.

Transcript panel: the words with timecodes; click to go there; select
words and press Delete to cut that range out of every unlocked track and
close up (extractRange).

Voice cleanup effects (Voice cleanup group): Reduce noise (afftdn on
export, gate and low cut preview), Voice isolation (no arnndn model is
bundled: afftdn, voice band, presence and compressor), Remove hum (50/60
Hz and harmonics, the same peaking cuts in both), De-ess (deesser on
export, split-band compressor preview), Loudness normalize (EBU R128
loudnorm on export, estimated while playing), and Duck under speech:
tracks marked Speech or Music; music ducks with sidechaincompress against
a speech stem on export and follows the speech tracks' level while
editing. Keyframed noise and hum amounts follow their keyframes on export
through asendcmd.
