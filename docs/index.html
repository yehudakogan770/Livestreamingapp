Everything you need
to go live.

From your first camera to a full multi-screen show. No extra apps, no plugins, no subscriptions.

Inputs

Every camera you own

Plug in USB webcams and capture cards, add IP and PTZ cameras, or turn any phone into a wireless camera.

Guests from anywhere

Send a link and up to 8 people join from their phone or computer. Nothing to install.

Videos, slides and more

Video playlists, pictures, PDFs, web pages, screen capture and song lyrics.

Scenes and layouts

Picture in picture, split screen and green screen, set up in a few clicks.

Produce

Switch like a pro

Line up every shot before it goes live. 22 transitions, stingers and a T-bar fader.

Cameras on autopilot

Let Lumora cut between your cameras every few seconds while you focus on the show.

Titles that look the part

Lower thirds, tickers and credits with 500 fonts and 14 animations, all in your event's colours.

Clean, balanced sound

A full audio mixer with noise removal, gate and compressor, and a limiter on every output.

Output

Stream everywhere at once

YouTube, Facebook and Vimeo are built in, plus any RTMP server. Up to 4K.

Record everything

Every event is saved to your laptop as it happens, with instant replay in slow motion.

Get the room involved

Polls, raffles and trivia played on people's own phones, with live results on the big screen.

Control it your way

Keyboard shortcuts, Stream Deck, MIDI controllers, presentation clickers or your phone.

Run every screen
in the room.

Other apps only handle the stream. Lumora also runs the projector behind the stage and the monitor your speakers read, so one person can run the whole event. Switch between them with F1, F2 and F3.

  1. 18:30

    Doors open

    A countdown fills the stream and the big screen. When it hits zero, Lumora goes straight to your next shot. No need to watch the clock.

    • Countdown timers
    • Video loops
    • Type the time to jump to
  2. 19:00

    Welcome

    Your first speaker walks on. Their name appears in your event's colours, and the monitor at their feet counts down their five minutes.

    • Event look
    • 500 fonts
    • 14 text effects
    • Stage monitor
  3. 19:12

    The performance

    Set three cameras to switch on their own while you keep an eye on the sound. Noise removal takes the hum and hiss out of the mics.

    • Limiter

    The year in photos

Stay calm when things go wrong.

Panic button
Double-click and every screen goes black instantly. One click brings it all back.
No error screens
If a camera drops out or a video fails, your audience sees your logo, never an error message.
Auto-save
Everything saves as you go. If the laptop restarts, your event comes back exactly as it was.
Works offline
No internet needed during your event, except to stream.
