One evening,
run from one computer.

Lumora is live production software for Windows. It switches your cameras, streams and records, and drives every screen in the room: the stream, the projector behind the stage and the monitor facing your speakers. Lumora Studio edits what you recorded. Lumora Planner keeps the team on one run of show.

This page follows one event, a school's spring gala, from the planning meeting to the edit the morning after. Each entry is a moment of the night and what Lumora does then.

Tue 4:00 PM

Tuesday, 4:00 PM. Plan with your team.

Lumora Planner runs in the browser, so the whole team works on the same run of show: sections, cues, start times, who is responsible, and notes, with comments on each cue. Start times and running totals are worked out for you, and you can print a cue sheet for the stage manager.

Invite a teammate by email. They make an account in the Planner and can start right away; teammates don't need approval and see only the plans shared with them. On the day, Lumora loads the plan as cues: Run of show, then Load from Planner.

Open Lumora Planner

Sat 5:30 PM

Saturday, 5:30 PM. Load-in.

Everything that can appear on a screen is an input: USB and capture-card cameras, NDI and stream sources, guests joining by link, screen capture, video files, slides, web pages, titles, scoreboards and countdowns. Microphones and the sound desk come in the same way and show up on the mixer.

The venue has no internet? That's fine. Switching, recording, titles and every screen in the room run on the computer; only streaming out and online inputs need a connection.

7:00 PM

7:00 PM. Doors.

Guests find their seats. The projector behind the stage, which Lumora calls the Back Screen, shows a countdown to the start while the stream shows the same clock. When it reaches zero, the countdown can take the first cue by itself.

Between segments, the Back Screen plays stage visuals: twenty scenes grouped by tempo that move with the beat of the music, with an energy control and an autopilot that changes scene every few bars.

7:30 PM

7:30 PM. On air.

You line up the next shot on the left, out of sight, and take it live on the right. One operator runs the stream, the recording, the Back Screen and the Stage Monitor from the same window.

Switching
Next and On air monitors, TAKE, CUT and a T-bar. 22 transitions and video stingers. Picture in picture, split screen, green screen.
Streaming
YouTube, Facebook, Vimeo, Twitch or any RTMP server, several at once, from 720p to 4K. A separate vertical 9:16 stream for phones. NDI out to the network.
Recording
The program and every camera to its own file, so the edit has all the angles. Instant replay with slow motion.
Control
Presets, cues and a timed run of show. A phone or tablet as a remote. Stream Deck plugin, MIDI controllers, presentation clickers and keyboard shortcuts.

7:34 PM

7:34 PM. Welcome.

The head of school walks to the podium. Her name and title come up on the stream as a lower third in your colors, fonts and logo, set once for every title, ticker and scoreboard. Lumora includes 500 open-source fonts that work offline, and you can add your own.

Captions are transcribed live, on the computer. The camera follows her as she moves with auto-framing, and background removal works without a green screen. The Stage Monitor in front of her shows the clock, the time left, messages from the booth, and a teleprompter.

8:15 PM

8:15 PM. The room joins in.

A code on the screen takes guests to a page on their phones. They vote in a poll, enter the raffle, play trivia, post to the messages wall, or pledge to the fundraiser, and the results appear on screen as they come in.

Phones can join the venue's own Wi-Fi, so this works even where there is no internet.

8:52 PM

8:52 PM. A camera cable gets kicked out.

The audience sees your logo, never an error message, and you switch to another camera. Lumora saves as you go: if the computer restarts, the event comes back as it was. And if something is really wrong, one button blanks every screen at once.

Sun 10:00 AM

Sunday, 10:00 AM. The edit, in Lumora Studio.

Open the gala's recording and every camera is already in sync, ready to cut like a multicam edit. Change any shot by picking another angle, tighten the speeches, fix the color and the sound, add captions, and export the full evening or a short vertical clip of the best moments.

Editing
Multi-track timeline with ripple, roll, slip and slide. Speed ramps, keyframes, titles, transitions and effects. Shared projects with comments.
Color
Node-based grading with wheels, curves and LUTs. Waveform, parade, vectorscope and histogram. Masks that find and follow people and objects.
Smart tools
Automatic multicam cutting, silence and filler word removal, reframing for vertical and square, and highlight reels from long recordings.
Captions
Transcripts you edit like text. Captions burned in, embedded, or as an SRT file. Proxies and a render cache for smooth playback.

Questions

Windows says it protected my PC. Is Lumora safe to run?

Windows SmartScreen warns about new apps it has not seen often. Click More info, then Run anyway. On Windows 11 computers with Smart App Control turned on, the installer may be blocked with no option to run it; Smart App Control can only be switched off as a whole, in Windows Security under App & browser control, so decide with whoever manages the computer. Only download Lumora from this page or the project's GitHub releases.

Why does my account need approval?

You create your account inside Lumora or Lumora Studio the first time you open it. Each new account is approved by the Lumora team for the apps it will use. Until then the app waits at the sign-in screen, and it opens once you are approved. Making plans in Lumora Planner also takes an approved account, but the teammates you share a plan with don't need approval: they make an account in the Planner and see only the plans shared with them.

Does Lumora work without the internet?

Yes. Once your account is approved, Lumora checks it whenever it starts with an internet connection, and without one it keeps opening for up to 7 days after the last check. Connect before the event and a venue without internet is not a problem. Switching, recording, titles and every screen in the room run on the computer. Only streaming out, live chat, guests by link and inputs from internet addresses need a connection. Phones for polls and the remote can join a local Wi-Fi network instead.

What is in an error report, and who sees it?

Nothing is sent unless you agree. If you say yes, reports of unexpected errors are cleaned of personal details before they are sent, and only the Lumora team can read them. Your event, project and media files are never included. You can change your answer at any time in Settings in Lumora, or the Help menu in Lumora Studio.

Does it work with Stream Deck and other controllers?

Yes. The Lumora Stream Deck plugin (see Download) puts TAKE, CUT, inputs with tally, overlays, replay, recording and going live on an Elgato Stream Deck. X-keys and other panels work through Bitfocus Companion, and Lumora also takes MIDI controllers, presentation clickers, and a phone or tablet on the same network.

Can I use my own fonts and branding?

Yes. Set your colors, fonts and logo once and every title, lower third, ticker and scoreboard follows it. Lumora includes 500 open-source fonts that work offline, and you can add your own.
