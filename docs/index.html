Run every screen at your event from one laptop.

Lumora is a Windows app for school shows, award nights, dinners, conferences, concerts and community events. It runs the live stream, the projector behind the stage and the monitor your speakers read, all at the same time.

Download for Windows Free. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Works without internet.

Three screens, each showing the right thing

Most events have the same three screens. Lumora knows what each one is for, so you never send the wrong picture to the wrong place.

Live Screen F1

The stream and the recording. Cameras, videos and titles go here. You line up the next shot where nobody sees it, then press TAKE.

Back Screen F2

The projector behind the stage. It can copy the Live Screen with one click, or show its own slides, video loops or visuals that move with the music.

Monitor F3

The screen facing the stage. Only words: a clock, a countdown, "5 minutes left", the next song line. The audience never sees it.

Titles that look like your event

Set your colours, fonts, logo and the box behind the words once, in Event look. Every name, lower third, song and scoreboard follows it, so the whole night matches without fiddling with each title.

Start from one of eight designs or change every detail. There are 500 fonts built in, and 14 ways for words to come on screen. Text always fades on and off cleanly.

What's in it

On the screens

  • 22 transitions, a fader, fade to black
  • Names, lower thirds, tickers and credits
  • 500 fonts built in, 14 ways for text to come on
  • One Event look for every title: colours, fonts, logo
  • Countdowns that go to the next item at zero
  • Slideshows and PDFs, song lyrics
  • Scoreboards with a game clock
  • Picture in picture, split screen, green screen

Cameras and sound

  • USB cameras, capture cards, IP and PTZ cameras
  • A phone as a camera, over your Wi-Fi
  • Camera buttons and an automatic camera switcher
  • Zoom, focus and exposure from the app
  • Guests join from a browser with a link
  • Sound mixer with noise removal, gate and compressor
  • Instant replay with slow motion

Running the event

  • Presets for each moment, in a run of show
  • Stream to several sites at once, up to 4K
  • Every event recorded to a file
  • Polls, raffles, trivia, auctions from the audience's phones
  • Run it from a phone, a Stream Deck, MIDI or a clicker
  • PANIC: one double-click and the audience sees nothing
  • If something breaks, the screens show your logo
  • The manual is inside the app (Help menu)

What you need

Windows
10 or 11, 64-bit
Computer
A recent laptop. 8 GB memory; 16 GB for 4K or lots of cameras.
Screens
Your laptop screen plus a projector, a stage monitor or a TV, by HDMI or a USB display adapter
Internet
Only for streaming

Help

The manual is in the app under Help, How to use Lumora. Type a word like "camera" or "stream" in its search box.
