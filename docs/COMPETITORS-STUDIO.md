# Lumora Studio against the other editors

What every editor that does some of Studio's job can do, what people
complain about in each, and Studio's answer. Researched October 2026 from the
makers' release notes (DaVinci Resolve 20, Premiere Pro 25/26, Final Cut Pro 11,
Avid Media Composer 2025, CapCut desktop, Descript, Riverside, Opus Clip,
Vizard, Submagic, Camtasia 2025, Filmora 14, Shotcut, Kdenlive, LumaFusion),
reviews, and editors' forums (r/editors, r/premiere, r/davinciresolve,
Blackmagic and Adobe forums, Trustpilot).

The goal from the owner: **every meaningful tool any of them has, Studio has
too, plus what none of them has**, polished, tested, and hidden until ready.

Key: ✅ has it · ◐ partly · ❌ doesn't · **new** = built in this round.

Columns: **St** Lumora Studio · **Re** DaVinci Resolve · **Pr** Premiere Pro ·
**FC** Final Cut Pro · **Av** Avid Media Composer · **CC** CapCut desktop ·
**De** Descript · **Ri** Riverside · **Op** Opus Clip / Vizard / Submagic ·
**Ca** Camtasia · **Fi** Filmora · **Sh** Shotcut · **Kd** Kdenlive ·
**LF** LumaFusion

to platforms other than YouTube, and client review links. Still ◐: a magnetic
timeline (ripple tools instead), who is speaking (by microphone, not by voice
on one microphone), voice isolation (filters, not a neural model), hand-drawn
masks (straight-sided, not animated), camera RAW (converted first) and the
native GPU engine (offered, not the default). See "What is left" below.

### Only in Studio

- **A whole recorded event opens as one edit** — every camera, every
  microphone, the live switch as the first cut, titles and replay markers
  where they happened, and the time of day on every frame.
- **Finish the event** — one click: cameras cut by who is talking, speech
  written down, captions, chapters, a highlight reel and framed, captioned
  clips for social, then everything queued (and the event ready to publish).
- **One title engine with the live show** — titles made in Lumora Titler look
  the same on air, in the edit and in every export; data, sounds and formats
  carry over.
- **Everything AI runs on this computer** — transcription, translation,
  masks, search, framing and clip finding cost nothing per minute, need no
  upload and keep working with no internet. (CapCut, Descript, Riverside and
  the clip tools charge credits or minutes and need the cloud.)
- **The YouTube channel is connected once for both apps** — Lumora's live
  streams and Studio's uploads use the same connection.
- **Send to Lumora** — a finished film goes straight into the live app's
  library for the next event.
- **Highlight reel built from the room** — applause, laughter and the live
  operator's replay markers find the moments.

## What is left, and why

| Tool                                                       | Why not yet                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Generative AI (extend, voices, dubbing, eye contact)       | Needs cloud models and paid generation; Studio keeps every AI tool on the computer, free and private.                                    |
| Automatic B-roll                                           | Needs a stock library, or a reliable index of what each of your own shots shows; media search is the start of it.                        |
| Who is speaking on a single microphone                     | Needs a voice model to tell voices apart; today speakers are told apart by microphone or file.                                           |
| A neural voice isolation model                             | Voice isolation uses noise-following filters today; a bundled model (as Resolve and Premiere ship) would be cleaner on hard recordings.  |
| Animated hand-drawn masks (rotoscoping)                    | Drawn masks are fixed shapes on the frame; points that move over time, or follow a track, are next.                                      |
| HDR grading and color management                           | HDR files are shown and exported as SDR today; a color-managed pipeline (Rec. 2020, PQ/HLG out) is a larger change to the compositor.    |
| VST plug-ins, a node compositor                            | Large native projects (a plug-in host; a node graph and its viewer).                                                                     |
| Publishing to TikTok, Facebook, Vimeo; client review links | Each platform needs its own app registration and review; client links need hosted video storage and a public review page.                |
| Native GPU engine as the default                           | It is offered on capable graphics cards and falls back to WebGL by itself; making it the default needs testing on real Windows machines. |
