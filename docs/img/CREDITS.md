# Image and video credits

The website's screenshots and the live demo use real footage of big live
events: an arena stage with LED walls and lighting rigs, a packed auditorium
and a speaker with a handheld microphone. The clips come from a few
photographers, cut together as if they were the cameras of one show. The event
name ("Northwind Summit 2026"), the speaker's name title ("Daniel Brooks, Chief
Product Officer") and the keynote slide are made up for the demo. Readable
banners and a shirt badge are blurred.

All footage is free to use commercially without attribution under the
[Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/); it is credited here anyway.
