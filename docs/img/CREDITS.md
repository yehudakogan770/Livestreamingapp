# Image and video credits

The website's screenshots and the live demo use real footage of one conference
(filmed as a series by the same photographer), so the cameras in Lumora and
Lumora Studio show the same event from different angles. The event name
("Northwind Summit 2026"), the speaker's name title ("Daniel Brooks, Chief
Product Officer") and the keynote slide are made up for the demo.

All footage is free to use commercially without attribution under its site's
license; it is credited here anyway.
