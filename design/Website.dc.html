WHAT LUMORA IS One control room for your whole event
Lumora is a Windows app for people who run live events — conferences, celebrations, school and community evenings, concerts. It shows the right picture on each screen, sends messages to the people on stage, streams to the internet and records everything, so one person can run the whole show calmly from a laptop.
